Operating lease schedule: Interest accretion for each period equals the operating lease discount rate multiplied by the average of the beginning and ending operating lease liability for that period. ROU asset amortization for each period equals the total lease cost for the period less the interest accretion, and the ROU asset end-of-period balance equals its beginning balance plus new lease additions less ROU amortization.

WACC debt inputs: The short-term bank credit / revolver, the current portion of long-term debt and finance leases, and the long-term debt and finance leases (excluding current portion) used in the WACC build are each the two-period average of the corresponding Financial Statement Model balance-sheet end-of-period balances (the average of the prior-period and current-period columns).

When circularity is on in the sheet “Financial Statement Model” the interest should be calculated by average-balance convention. The applicable interest base should be the average of the prior-period ending balance and current-period ending balance for the relevant balance sheet line item(s).
