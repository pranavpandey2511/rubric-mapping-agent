**Textron - Instructions 1**

We are evaluating a potential leveraged buyout (LBO) of Textron Inc. It is a US-based multi-industry company that operates across several major segments including aerospace (through brands like Cessna and Beechcraft), defense (via Bell and Textron Systems), industrial vehicles and systems, and finance. Its portfolio includes aircraft, helicopters and tilt-rotors, unmanned systems, weapons and sensor systems, light utility and turf vehicles, fuel and fluid systems for the automotive industry, and related services. As the first step in evaluation process, we need to first build a 3-statement financial model for Textron.

Your task (work to perform):

- Spread historical financials in the ‘Operating_Model’ sheet of the template for the past three fiscal years (2022-2024).

- The template line items may not directly align with those in Textron’s Income Statement, Balance Sheet, or Cash Flow Statement. Review supplementary notes and management discussions in the annual filings to make the necessary adjustments, ensuring that key figures such as Total Assets, Net Income, CFO, CFI, CFF reconcile accurately. Key adjustments required:

  - Combine common line items for the company’s Manufacturing group and Finance group.

    - Other assets and Other liabilities of Finance Group should be considered as non-current.

  - Deferred taxes: Separate deferred tax assets and deferred tax liabilities from their existing categories and report a net value as “Deferred tax assets, net”.

  - Debt classification: Combine current maturities of long-term debt with long-term debt under non-current liabilities.

  - Operating leases: Present operating lease assets and liabilities as separate line items on the balance sheet. Include related cost in the income statement based on supplementary footnotes. Assume that this cost was originally part of Selling and Administrative expenses.

- Develop a fully interlinked three-statement model using the historical financials and the operating drivers (provided in the ‘Business_Assumptions’ excel file).

  - Multiple scenario assumptions have been provided for the revenue % growth of Textron’s Aviation, Bell, Systems and Industrial segments.

  - Note that the company is shutting down its eAviation segment from the beginning of forecast period.

  - Finance Revenues should be projected to grow in line with total operating revenues over the forecast period.

  - Lease schedule is to be prepared for the forecast period only.
